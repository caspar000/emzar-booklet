| The heroes must find out the truth about the blessed totem or the criminal will besiege the castle.                    |

Zuzik, a disgruntled monk tired of his duties, on his way to conduct a celebration in Rodorf found a killed Orc party. Among the corpses, he discovered a Totem which he recognized from his arcane studies as a Totem for the Goblin God (+6 persuasion towards Goblins). Ecstatic, he decided to abandon his duty towards the village and to find some Goblins so he could convince them to join him. 

Zuzik succeeded in persuading the Goblins. He set his sights towards a small castle on a hill not so far from here. He would kill what little guards the castle head and use his newly acquired Goblin forces to rebuild and expand from a favorable position.

---

Players will start the session separately. All of them will have to roll the dice to decide where they start the session from. Two of them will be in a cage, others will be outside. They will have to work together to overcome this encounter, after which they should band together as a party.

If players trust Zuzik, he will try to convince them to attack the abandoned castle and the Guards, making the out to be Bandits who robbed him and took all his gear required to conduct the celebration in the town. If the players follow Zuziks plan, he will try to get rid of them in the castle after they take damage from the Guards.

If players don't trust Zuzik, he will try to use his "Glue Bomb" and "Potion of Emergency Escape" to run to the castle guards and convince them that the players are bandits trying to kill him. He will have an advantage since he is a respected monk.
